“京剧以其独特的艺术风格和表演形式闻名，被誉为中国的国粹，在世界舞台上产生了深远影响。”
▶ 自己翻完了？点开对照
信息块拆解
  1. 特征（独特的艺术风格）→ with its unique artistic style
  2. 评价（被誉为国粹）→ is hailed as
  3. 影响（世界舞台）→ has exerted a far-reaching influence
Peking Opera, with its unique artistic style and performance forms, is hailed as a national treasure of China and has exerted a far-reaching influence on the world stage.
