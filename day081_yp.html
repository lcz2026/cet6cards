📖 今日翻译押题 · 汉字是世界上历史最悠久的文…
王江涛押题 · 2026.12 热点 · 先动笔翻再对照，5 分钟过完
汉字（Chinese characters）是世界上历史最悠久的文字之一，已有数千年的历史。从甲骨文到今天的简体字，汉字的形态不断演变，但始终承载着中华文明。书法（calligraphy）是以汉字为载体的传统艺术，讲究笔法、结构和章法。自古以来，书法家通过笔墨表达情感与思想，留下了许多传世名作。如今，随着信息技术的发展，人们越来越多地用键盘输入代替手写，汉字书写能力有所下降。为此，许多学校开设了书法课程，鼓励年轻人拿起毛笔，感受汉字之美。相信在他们的努力下，这一独特的文化艺术将代代相传，焕发新的光彩。

第 1 句
汉字（Chinese characters）是世界上历史最悠久的文字之一，已有数千年的历史。
Chinese characters are among the oldest writing systems in the world, with a history of thousands of years.
第 2 句
从甲骨文到今天的简体字，汉字的形态不断演变，但始终承载着中华文明。
From oracle bone script to today's simplified characters, the forms of Chinese characters have kept changing, but they have always carried Chinese civilization.
第 3 句
书法（calligraphy）是以汉字为载体的传统艺术，讲究笔法、结构和章法。
Calligraphy is a traditional art based on Chinese characters, which pays attention to stroke methods, structure and composition.
第 4 句
自古以来，书法家通过笔墨表达情感与思想，留下了许多传世名作。
Since ancient times, calligraphers have expressed their feelings and thoughts through brush and ink, leaving behind many famous works.
第 5 句
如今，随着信息技术的发展，人们越来越多地用键盘输入代替手写，汉字书写能力有所下降。
Nowadays, with the development of information technology, people increasingly use keyboards instead of handwriting, and the ability to write Chinese characters has declined.
第 6 句
为此，许多学校开设了书法课程，鼓励年轻人拿起毛笔，感受汉字之美。
Therefore, many schools have set up calligraphy courses, encouraging young people to pick up the brush and feel the beauty of Chinese characters.
第 7 句
相信在他们的努力下，这一独特的文化艺术将代代相传，焕发新的光彩。
With their efforts, this unique culture and art will be passed down from generation to generation and shine with new glory.

✍️ 今日作文押题 · 负责任地使用人工智能
王江涛 12 月预测 · 指定首句作文（6 月三套同款命题形态）
指定首句：As AI is used more and more extensively, there is a growing awareness of the importance of using it properly and responsibly.
👆 范文开头段 · 点遮英文自测
① 开头段
Artificial intelligence is reshaping the way we learn and live. From homework helpers to instant translation, AI has become a study partner for many students. In my view, AI should serve as a learning aid rather than a shortcut, and only responsible use can turn it into a real friend of learning.
人工智能正在重塑我们的学习和生活方式。从作业助手到即时翻译，AI 已成为许多学生的学习伙伴。在我看来，AI 应该是学习辅助工具而不是捷径，只有负责任地使用它，才能让它成为学习真正的朋友。

① 模板开头 · 首句照抄+亮观点
As AI is used more and more extensively, there is a growing awareness of the importance of using it properly and responsibly. In an increasingly interconnected world, responsible AI use stands out as an essential priority. It is of great necessity for students and young people to equip themselves with practical skills and experience. Reasons and concrete evidence to support my viewpoint are as follows.
负责任地使用人工智能。（首句照抄）在这个联系日益紧密的世界里，负责任地使用 AI脱颖而出，成为一项必不可少的优先事项。学生和年轻人有必要让自己掌握实用技能与经验。支持我观点的理由和具体证据如下。
② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, acquiring fundamental knowledge of responsible AI use yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to working out key steps by ourselves. Moreover, responsible AI use is instrumental in promoting physical and mental health. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of engaging with responsible AI use cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，掌握负责任地使用 AI的基础知识会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升个人前景与核心能力上。其中最主要的一点，是它对自己理清关键步骤有显著贡献。此外，负责任地使用 AI有助于促进身心健康。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，参与负责任地使用 AI的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that responsible AI use is of great importance for the whole society. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. In the future, we should continue to explore and develop responsible AI use, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
简而言之，负责任地使用 AI对整个社会的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续探索和发展负责任地使用 AI，让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
