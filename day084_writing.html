① 开头段
Short videos have swept into every corner of our daily life. They bring laughter and knowledge within a tap of the screen. Yet whether they serve us or consume us depends on one key factor: our self-discipline.
短视频已经席卷了我们日常生活的每个角落。只需轻点屏幕，它们就能带来欢笑和知识。然而，它们究竟是服务于我们还是消耗我们，取决于一个关键因素：我们的自律。
② 主体段
On the one hand, short videos lower the threshold of knowledge: people can learn cooking, science and languages within minutes, and small businesses can reach customers through creative short clips. On the other hand, their addictive nature leads to endless scrolling, which wastes fragmented time and gradually weakens our attention spans.
一方面，短视频降低了获取知识的门槛：人们可以在几分钟内学会烹饪、科学和语言知识，小微企业也能通过有创意的短视频触达顾客。另一方面，短视频的成瘾性导致无休止地刷屏，浪费了碎片时间，也在逐渐削弱我们的注意力。
③ 结尾段
In conclusion, short videos are a double-edged sword. With clear time limits and purposeful watching, we can enjoy their benefits while avoiding their traps; without self-discipline, however, we may become prisoners of the screen.
总之，短视频是一把双刃剑。设定明确的时间限制、带着目的去观看，我们就能享受它的益处同时避开陷阱；但若没有自律，我们就可能沦为屏幕的囚徒。
