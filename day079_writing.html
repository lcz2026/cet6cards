① 开头 · 引题亮观点
Nowadays, environmental problems such as air pollution and waste are getting worse. It is high time that we lived a green lifestyle, because protecting the environment is everyone's responsibility.
如今，空气污染和浪费等环境问题日益严重。我们是时候践行绿色生活方式了，因为保护环境是每个人的责任。
② 主体段 · 怎么做
Living green is not difficult. First, we can take buses or ride bikes instead of driving cars, which helps cut down air pollution. Second, we should say no to waste: turn off the lights when leaving a room, and reuse paper and plastic bags. Small actions like these can make a big difference.
绿色生活并不难。第一，我们可以乘坐公交车或骑自行车代替开车，这有助于减少空气污染。第二，我们应该对浪费说“不”：离开房间时随手关灯，重复使用纸张和塑料袋。这样的小行动能带来大改变。
③ 结尾 · 重申+展望
In conclusion, a green lifestyle benefits both nature and ourselves. If everyone does his part, our world will become cleaner and more beautiful.
总之，绿色生活方式对自然和我们自己都有益处。如果人人都尽自己的一份力，我们的世界将变得更加清洁、更加美丽。

① 模板开头 · 题干句+亮观点
Green lifestyle is everyone's responsibility. In an increasingly interconnected world, living a green lifestyle stands out as an essential priority. It is of great necessity for every citizen to equip themselves with the sense of environmental responsibility. Reasons and concrete evidence to support my viewpoint are as follows.
绿色生活方式是每个人的责任。（题干句照抄）在这个联系日益紧密的世界里，践行绿色生活成为一项必不可少的优先事项。对每个公民来说，用环保责任感武装自己是大有必要的。支持我观点的理由和具体证据如下。
② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, living green yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in keeping us healthy and saving daily costs: taking buses instead of driving and saying no to waste are all good habits. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to social well-being and sustainable development. Moreover, a green lifestyle is instrumental in building a greener society: small actions like reusing paper and recycling plastic bags can make a big difference. This not only enhances the public's awareness of joint responsibility but also fosters a healthier and more harmonious society. Last but not least, the process of protecting the environment cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，绿色生活会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在保持健康与节省日常开支上：乘公交代替开车、对浪费说不，都是好习惯。其中最主要的收益，是它对社会福祉与可持续发展有显著贡献。此外，绿色生活方式有助于建设更绿色的社会：重复使用纸张、回收塑料袋这样的小行动能带来大改变。这不仅增强了公众共同担责的意识，也促进了一个更健康、更和谐的社会。最后同样重要的是，保护环境的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that living a green lifestyle is of great importance for our planet. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. In the future, we should continue to explore and develop greener ways of living, making our world shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
简而言之，绿色生活对我们星球的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续探索和发展更绿色的生活方式，让我们的世界在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
