① 开头段
Living in a sea of information, young people need digital literacy more than ever. Every day we receive countless messages, but not all of them deserve our trust. Knowing how to find, judge and use information has become a basic survival skill.
生活在信息的海洋中，年轻人比以往任何时候都更需要数字素养。我们每天接收无数信息，但并非所有信息都值得信任。知道如何查找、判断和使用信息，已成为一项基本的生存技能。
② 主体段
First and foremost, misinformation spreads faster than facts online, and only careful readers can tell rumors from reality. What's more, algorithms feed us what we already like, building invisible information bubbles that narrow our view of the world. Last but not least, digital literacy also means self-protection: guarding personal privacy and resisting online fraud.
首先，虚假信息在网上比事实传播得更快，只有细心的读者才能分辨谣言与现实。其次，算法只推送我们喜欢的内容，形成看不见的信息茧房，让我们的视野变窄。最后，数字素养也意味着自我保护：守护个人隐私、抵御网络诈骗。
③ 结尾段
In conclusion, digital literacy is the new reading and writing of our era. Schools should teach it, platforms should support it, and above all, every young user should practice it—checking sources, questioning bias and thinking before sharing.
总之，数字素养是当代新的「读」与「写」。学校应当教授它，平台应当支持它，而最重要的是，每个年轻用户都应践行它——核查来源、质疑偏见、三思而后转发。

② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, learning to tell facts from rumors yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in protecting ourselves from misinformation and fraud. Chiefly among these benefits is the ability to check sources and question bias. Moreover, digital literacy is instrumental in promoting rational thinking and online safety. Last but not least, thinking before sharing cultivates a more responsible online habit, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，学会分辨事实与谣言会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在保护自己远离虚假信息与诈骗上。其中最主要的一点，是核查来源、质疑偏见的能力。此外，数字素养有助于促进理性思考与上网安全。这不仅拓宽我们看世界的视野，还营造更健康的网络环境。最后同样重要的是，转发前先思考能养成更有担当的上网习惯，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
