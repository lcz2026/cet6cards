① 开头 · 引名言点题
There is an old saying that constant dripping wears away the stone. It tells us that the way to success lies in perseverance.
有句老话说，水滴石穿。它告诉我们：成功之路在于坚持。
② 主体段 · 名人例证
On the road to success, everyone meets difficulties. What separates winners from losers is not talent but persistence. Thomas Edison failed thousands of times before he invented the light bulb. It was perseverance that turned his dream into reality. Without it, even the most talented person can achieve nothing.
在通往成功的路上，人人都会遇到困难。把赢家和输家区分开的不是天赋，而是坚持。爱迪生发明灯泡前失败了上千次。正是坚持把梦想变成了现实。没有坚持，再有天赋的人也将一事无成。
③ 结尾 · 重申+展望
In conclusion, never give up when things get hard. As long as we hold on to our goals, success will come sooner or later.
总之，困难面前永不放弃。只要坚持目标，成功或早或晚都会到来。

Constant dripping wears away the stone. In an increasingly interconnected world, perseverance stands out as an essential priority. It is of great necessity for young people to equip themselves with this precious quality. Reasons and concrete evidence to support my viewpoint are as follows.

② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, cultivating perseverance yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to boosting employability and career advancement, for employers always favor those who never quit halfway. Moreover, perseverance is instrumental in promoting academic progress and achievement: Thomas Edison failed thousands of times before inventing the light bulb, and it was persistence that turned his dream into reality. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of sticking to our goals cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，培养坚持的品质会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升未来前景与核心能力上。其中最主要的一点，是它对提升就业竞争力与职业晋升有显著贡献，因为雇主总是青睐从不半途而废的人。此外，坚持有助于促进学业进步与成绩提升：爱迪生发明灯泡前失败了上千次，正是毅力把梦想变成了现实。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，坚持目标的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that perseverance is of great importance for our growth. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. In the future, we should continue to explore and develop this quality, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
简而言之，坚持对我们成长的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续磨炼和培养这一品质，让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
