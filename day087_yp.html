Exploring outer space has always been a cherished dream of the Chinese nation. In 2003, the successful launch of the Shenzhou 5 spacecraft marked a historic moment as Yang Liwei became China's first astronaut to venture into space. In 2008, the Shenzhou 7 mission saw Zhai Zhigang become the first Chinese astronaut to perform a spacewalk. In recent years, China’s space exploration has entered a phase of rapid innovation and development, with steady progress in building space infrastructure. The construction of the Chinese Space Station was fully completed in 2022. The rapid advancement of China's space industry has written a glorious chapter in the history of the Chinese nation and made significant contributions to the progress of human civilization. In the future, China’s journey to explore the cosmos will be more stable and far-reaching.
