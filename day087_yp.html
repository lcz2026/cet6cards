📖 今日翻译押题 · 长征是中国共产党领导的中国…
瑞斯拜12月版 · 2026.12 热点 · 先动笔翻再对照，5 分钟过完
长征是中国共产党领导的中国工农红军在1934年至1936年间进行的一次伟大战略转移。长征途中，红军将士穿越了荒无人烟的雪山草地，跨越了十余个省，行程约两万五千里。长征的胜利，不仅保存了中国革命的有生力量，更铸就了伟大的长征精神。这种精神是中华民族百折不挠的民族精神的最高体现，也是激励中国人民不断前进的强大力量。如今，长征精神依然激励着中国人克服各种困难，为实现中华民族的伟大复兴而努力奋斗。

第 1 句
长征是中国共产党领导的中国工农红军在1934年至1936年间进行的一次伟大战略转移。
The Long March was a great strategic transfer carried out between 1934 and 1936 by the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army under the leadership of the Communist Party of China.
第 2 句
长征途中，红军将士穿越了荒无人烟的雪山草地，跨越了十余个省，行程约两万五千里。
On the way, the Red Army soldiers crossed uninhabited snow-capped mountains and marshlands, passed through more than ten provinces and covered a distance of about 25,000 li.
第 3 句
长征的胜利，不仅保存了中国革命的有生力量，更铸就了伟大的长征精神。
The victory of the Long March not only preserved the effective forces of the Chinese revolution but also forged the great Long March spirit.
第 4 句
这种精神是中华民族百折不挠的民族精神的最高体现，也是激励中国人民不断前进的强大力量。
This spirit is the highest embodiment of the Chinese nation's indomitable character and a powerful force that inspires the Chinese people to keep moving forward.
第 5 句
如今，长征精神依然激励着中国人克服各种困难，为实现中华民族的伟大复兴而努力奋斗。
Today, the Long March spirit still inspires the Chinese people to overcome all kinds of difficulties and strive for the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation.

✍️ 今日作文押题 · 经济发展与环境保护
王江涛 12 月预测 · 指定首句作文（6 月三套同款命题形态）
指定首句：Today there is a growing awareness that environmental protection needs to be given as much attention as economic growth.
👆 范文开头段 · 点遮英文自测
① 开头段
Economic development and environmental protection are not enemies but partners. In the past, many countries grew rich at the cost of clear skies and clean rivers. Today, sustainable development shows us a wiser path.
经济发展与环境保护不是敌人，而是伙伴。过去，许多国家以失去蓝天清河为代价致富。今天，可持续发展为我们指明了一条更明智的道路。
⭐ 高分表达（混个眼熟）
sustainable development可持续发展carbon neutrality碳中和renewable energy可再生能源circular economy循环经济

① 模板开头 · 首句照抄+亮观点
Today there is a growing awareness that environmental protection needs to be given as much attention as economic growth. In an increasingly interconnected world, green development stands out as an essential priority. It is of great necessity for students and young people to equip themselves with practical skills and experience. Reasons and concrete evidence to support my viewpoint are as follows.
经济发展与环境保护。（首句照抄）在这个联系日益紧密的世界里，绿色发展脱颖而出，成为一项必不可少的优先事项。学生和年轻人有必要让自己掌握实用技能与经验。支持我观点的理由和具体证据如下。
② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, acquiring fundamental knowledge of green development yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to reducing pollution at its source. Moreover, green development is instrumental in promoting a sustainable economy. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of engaging with green development cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，掌握绿色发展的基础知识会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升个人前景与核心能力上。其中最主要的一点，是它对从源头减少污染有显著贡献。此外，绿色发展有助于促进可持续的经济。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，参与绿色发展的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that green development is of great importance for students and the whole society. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. In the future, we should continue to explore and develop green development, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
简而言之，绿色发展对学生与整个社会的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续探索和发展绿色发展，让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
