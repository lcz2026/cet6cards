📖 今日翻译押题 · 中国传统家训记录了长辈对后…
瑞斯拜12月版 · 2026.12 热点 · 先动笔翻再对照，5 分钟过完
中国传统家训记录了长辈对后代的期望，内容常涉及勤奋、诚信、节俭和待人处事。它们既是家庭教育材料，也为了解过去的社会生活提供了线索。不同家训产生于不同环境，其中某些观念具有时代局限，不能不加分析地应用于今天。现代家庭在借鉴传统时，可以重视尊重他人、承担责任等原则，同时鼓励孩子独立思考和表达意见。家庭教育的效果不仅取决于说了什么，也取决于成年人如何在生活中示范。

第 1 句
中国传统家训记录了长辈对后代的期望，内容常涉及勤奋、诚信、节俭和待人处事。
Traditional Chinese family precepts record the expectations that elders held for later generations, and their content often concerns diligence, integrity, thrift and how to deal with others.
第 2 句
它们既是家庭教育材料，也为了解过去的社会生活提供了线索。
They are both materials for family education and clues for understanding social life in the past.
第 3 句
不同家训产生于不同环境，其中某些观念具有时代局限，不能不加分析地应用于今天。
Different precepts emerged in different circumstances, and some of the ideas in them bear the limitations of their times, so they cannot be applied to the present without analysis.
第 4 句
现代家庭在借鉴传统时，可以重视尊重他人、承担责任等原则，同时鼓励孩子独立思考和表达意见。
In drawing on tradition, modern families can value principles such as respecting others and taking responsibility, while encouraging children to think independently and express their views.
第 5 句
家庭教育的效果不仅取决于说了什么，也取决于成年人如何在生活中示范。
The effectiveness of family education depends not only on what is said but also on how adults set an example in daily life.

✍️ 今日作文押题 · 当传统文化遇上科技
王江涛 12 月预测 · 指定首句作文（6 月三套同款命题形态）
指定首句：In an Era of Artificial Intelligence, traditional Chinese culture is becoming increasingly crucial for our social development.
👆 范文开头段 · 点遮英文自测
① 开头段
Modern technology is opening new doors for traditional culture. Centuries-old skills are no longer locked in museums; they are being digitized, shared and reinvented online. Tradition and technology, once seen as opposites, are now powerful partners.
现代科技正在为传统文化打开新的大门。数百年的技艺不再锁在博物馆里；它们正被数字化、被分享、在线上被重新演绎。传统与科技曾被视为对立面，如今成了强大的伙伴。

① 模板开头 · 首句照抄+亮观点
In an Era of Artificial Intelligence, traditional Chinese culture is becoming increasingly crucial for our social development. In an increasingly interconnected world, traditional culture stands out as an essential priority. It is of great necessity for students and young people to equip themselves with practical skills and experience. Reasons and concrete evidence to support my viewpoint are as follows.
当传统文化遇上科技。（首句照抄）在这个联系日益紧密的世界里，传统文化脱颖而出，成为一项必不可少的优先事项。学生和年轻人有必要让自己掌握实用技能与经验。支持我观点的理由和具体证据如下。
② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, acquiring fundamental knowledge of traditional culture yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to keeping old stories alive. Moreover, traditional culture is instrumental in promoting cultural confidence among the young. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of engaging with traditional culture cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，掌握传统文化的基础知识会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升个人前景与核心能力上。其中最主要的一点，是它对让老故事保持生命力有显著贡献。此外，传统文化有助于促进年轻一代的文化自信。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，参与传统文化的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that traditional culture is of great importance for students and the whole society. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. In the future, we should continue to explore and develop traditional culture, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
简而言之，传统文化对学生与整个社会的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续探索和发展传统文化，让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
