① 开头 · 现象+亮观点
Nowadays, short videos and social media have taken up much of our attention. In my opinion, deep reading in the digital age is more important than ever before.
如今，短视频和社交媒体占据了我们大量注意力。在我看来，数字时代的深度阅读比以往任何时候都重要。
② 主体段 · 让步+对比论证
Admittedly, digital reading is fast and convenient, for we can get information anytime and anywhere. However, fragmented reading hardly trains our minds. Deep reading, by contrast, teaches us to think logically and to focus on one thing for a long time. It also helps us better understand others' feelings.
诚然，数字阅读快速便捷，我们随时随地都能获取信息。然而，碎片化阅读很难锻炼思维。相比之下，深度阅读教会我们逻辑思考、长时间专注于一件事，还能让我们更好地理解他人的感受。
③ 结尾 · 建议+展望
Therefore, why not set aside 30 minutes every day for a paper book? Deep reading will keep our minds sharp in this fast-changing digital world.
因此，何不每天留出 30 分钟读一本纸质书呢？在这个快速变化的数字世界，深度阅读会让我们的头脑保持敏锐。

Reading never goes out of style. In an increasingly interconnected world, deep reading stands out as an essential priority. It is of great necessity for us to slow down and read a paper book carefully. Reasons and concrete evidence to support my viewpoint are as follows.
阅读永远不会过时。（题干句照抄）在这个联系日益紧密的世界里，深度阅读脱颖而出，成为一项必不可少的优先事项。慢下来认真读一本纸质书，是大有必要的。支持我观点的理由和具体证据如下。
② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, acquiring fundamental knowledge through deep reading yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to boosting employability and career advancement, because those who think logically are always favored by employers. Moreover, deep reading is instrumental in promoting academic progress and achievement: it trains us to focus on one thing for a long time instead of jumping between short videos. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of engaging with great books cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，通过深度阅读掌握基础知识，会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升未来前景与核心能力上。其中最主要的一点，是它对提升就业竞争力与职业晋升有显著贡献，因为逻辑思维强的人总是更受雇主青睐。此外，深度阅读有助于促进学业进步与成绩提升：它训练我们长时间专注于一件事，而不是在短视频之间跳来跳去。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，阅读伟大作品的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that deep reading is of great importance for our minds. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. In the future, we should continue to explore and develop the habit of deep reading, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
简而言之，深度阅读对我们头脑的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续培养和发展深度阅读的习惯，让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
