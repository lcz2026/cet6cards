① 开头 · 引谚语亮观点
As the saying goes, "Teamwork makes the dream work." No one can achieve great success alone, so learning to cooperate with others is a lesson we must learn.
俗话说：“团队合作成就梦想。”没有人能独自取得巨大成功，因此学会与他人合作是我们的必修课。
② 主体段 · 正反+举例
On the one hand, everyone has strengths and weaknesses. In a team, members can complement each other, so one plus one is often more than two. On the other hand, those who work alone are limited by their own abilities. Take a basketball game as an example: no star player can win without the passing and defense of his teammates.
一方面，人人都有长处和短处。在团队中，成员可以互补，一加一往往大于二。另一方面，单打独斗的人会被自身能力所限制。以篮球赛为例：没有队友的传球和防守，再耀眼的球星也无法赢球。
③ 结尾 · 重申+呼吁
In conclusion, teamwork is not only the key to success but also a core quality in the workplace. Let's learn to trust our partners and grow together.
总之，团队合作不仅是成功的钥匙，也是职场的核心素质。让我们学会信任伙伴，共同成长。
