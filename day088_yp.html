✍️ 今日作文押题 · 信息时代文科的价值
王江涛 12 月预测 · 指定首句作文（6 月三套同款命题形态）
指定首句：In an Information Era, liberal arts is becoming increasingly crucial for social development.
👆 范文开头段 · 点遮英文自测
① 开头段
In the information age, the value of the humanities deserves renewed attention. While technology determines how fast we run, the humanities decide where we are heading. Ignoring them would leave our society efficient but directionless.
在信息时代，人文学科的价值值得重新审视。技术决定我们跑得多快，而人文学科决定我们走向何方。忽视它们，我们的社会将变得高效却迷失方向。

① 模板开头 · 首句照抄+亮观点
In an Information Era, liberal arts is becoming increasingly crucial for social development. In an increasingly interconnected world, the study of the humanities stands out as an essential priority. It is of great necessity for students and young people to equip themselves with practical skills and experience. Reasons and concrete evidence to support my viewpoint are as follows.
信息时代文科的价值。（首句照抄）在这个联系日益紧密的世界里，人文学科的学习脱颖而出，成为一项必不可少的优先事项。学生和年轻人有必要让自己掌握实用技能与经验。支持我观点的理由和具体证据如下。
② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, acquiring fundamental knowledge of the study of the humanities yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to the soft skills machines cannot replace. Moreover, the study of the humanities is instrumental in promoting empathy, inclusion and humanistic wisdom. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of engaging with the study of the humanities cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，掌握人文学科的学习的基础知识会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升个人前景与核心能力上。其中最主要的一点，是它对机器无法替代的软技能有显著贡献。此外，人文学科的学习有助于促进同理心、包容性与人文智慧。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，参与人文学科的学习的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that the study of the humanities is of great importance for humanistic thinking. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. In the future, we should continue to explore and develop the study of the humanities, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
简而言之，人文学科的学习对人文思考的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续探索和发展人文学科的学习，让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
