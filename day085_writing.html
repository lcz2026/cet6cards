① 开头 · 现象+亮观点
Many students either study all day or play all day. As far as I am concerned, the wise way is to keep a balance between study and leisure.
许多学生要么整天学习，要么整天玩乐。在我看来，明智的做法是在学习与休闲之间保持平衡。
② 主体段 · 两面论证
On the one hand, studying without rest lowers efficiency, because our brains need relaxation to stay energetic. On the other hand, playing without limits makes our goals drift further and further away. Proper leisure, such as sports and music, actually recharges us for better learning.
一方面，不间断地学习会降低效率，因为大脑需要放松来保持活力。另一方面，无节制的玩乐会让目标渐行渐远。适当的休闲，比如运动和音乐，实际上是在为更好的学习充电蓄能。
③ 结尾 · 比喻收束
In a word, rest is not a waste of time but fuel for progress. Let's leave some blank space in our schedule for both study and play.
总之，休息不是浪费时间，而是进步的燃料。让我们在日程表里为学习与玩乐都留一些空白吧。

① 模板开头 · 题干句+亮观点
All work and no play makes Jack a dull boy. In an increasingly interconnected world, balancing study and leisure stands out as an essential priority. It is of great necessity for students to learn how to manage their time wisely. Reasons and concrete evidence to support my viewpoint are as follows.
只学习不玩耍，聪明孩子也变傻。（题干句照抄）在这个联系日益紧密的世界里，平衡学习与休闲成为一项必不可少的优先事项。对学生来说，学会明智地管理时间是大有必要的。支持我观点的理由和具体证据如下。
② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, arranging proper leisure yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies, because our brains need relaxation to stay energetic. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to boosting employability and career advancement: people who know when to rest seldom burn out and always work efficiently. Moreover, keeping a balance is instrumental in promoting academic progress and achievement, while playing without limits makes our goals drift further and further away. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of managing our own schedule cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，安排适当的休闲会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升未来前景与核心能力上，因为大脑需要放松才能保持活力。其中最主要的一点，是它对提升就业竞争力与职业晋升有显著贡献：懂得休息的人很少倦怠，总是高效工作。此外，保持平衡有助于促进学业进步与成绩提升，而无节制的玩乐只会让目标渐行渐远。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，管理自己日程的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that balancing study and leisure is of great importance for every learner. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. In the future, we should continue to explore and develop our own rhythm of work and rest, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
简而言之，平衡学习与休闲对每个学习者的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续探索和形成自己的劳逸节奏，让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
