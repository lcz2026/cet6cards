① 开头 · 引名言点题
There is an old saying that constant dripping wears away the stone. It tells us that the way to success lies in perseverance.
有句老话说，水滴石穿。它告诉我们：成功之路在于坚持。
② 主体段 · 名人例证
On the road to success, everyone meets difficulties. What separates winners from losers is not talent but persistence. Thomas Edison failed thousands of times before he invented the light bulb. It was perseverance that turned his dream into reality. Without it, even the most talented person can achieve nothing.
在通往成功的路上，人人都会遇到困难。把赢家和输家区分开的不是天赋，而是坚持。爱迪生发明灯泡前失败了上千次。正是坚持把梦想变成了现实。没有坚持，再有天赋的人也将一事无成。
③ 结尾 · 重申+展望
In conclusion, never give up when things get hard. As long as we hold on to our goals, success will come sooner or later.
总之，困难面前永不放弃。只要坚持目标，成功或早或晚都会到来。
