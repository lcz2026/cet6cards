① 开头 · 引题亮观点
Nowadays, environmental problems such as air pollution and waste are getting worse. It is high time that we lived a green lifestyle, because protecting the environment is everyone's responsibility.
如今，空气污染和浪费等环境问题日益严重。我们是时候践行绿色生活方式了，因为保护环境是每个人的责任。
② 主体段 · 怎么做
Living green is not difficult. First, we can take buses or ride bikes instead of driving cars, which helps cut down air pollution. Second, we should say no to waste: turn off the lights when leaving a room, and reuse paper and plastic bags. Small actions like these can make a big difference.
绿色生活并不难。第一，我们可以乘坐公交车或骑自行车代替开车，这有助于减少空气污染。第二，我们应该对浪费说“不”：离开房间时随手关灯，重复使用纸张和塑料袋。这样的小行动能带来大改变。
③ 结尾 · 重申+展望
In conclusion, a green lifestyle benefits both nature and ourselves. If everyone does his part, our world will become cleaner and more beautiful.
总之，绿色生活方式对自然和我们自己都有益处。如果人人都尽自己的一份力，我们的世界将变得更加清洁、更加美丽。
