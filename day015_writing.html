① 开头 · 引题亮观点
With the rapid development of the Internet, social media has changed the way we communicate. In my opinion, it brings people closer, but it also has some negative effects.
随着互联网的快速发展，社交媒体改变了我们的交流方式。在我看来，它拉近了人与人的距离，但也带来了一些负面影响。
② 主体段 · 正反对比
On the one hand, social media makes it easy to keep in touch with friends far away. A quick message or a video call can shorten the distance between people. On the other hand, many people are addicted to their phones, so face-to-face communication is becoming less and less. Some even feel lonelier though they have hundreds of "friends" online.
一方面，社交媒体让我们与远方的朋友保持联系变得容易。一条简讯或一次视频通话就能缩短人与人之间的距离。另一方面，许多人沉迷手机，面对面的交流越来越少。有些人网上有几百个“好友”，却反而感到更孤独。
③ 结尾 · 重申+展望
In conclusion, social media is just a tool. We should use it wisely and never let it replace real-life communication. Only in this way can we enjoy both technology and true friendship.
总之，社交媒体只是一个工具。我们应当明智地使用它，绝不能让它取代现实生活中的交流。只有这样，我们才能同时拥有科技与真挚的友谊。
