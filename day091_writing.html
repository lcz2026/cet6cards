① 开头段
Modern technology is opening new doors for traditional culture. Centuries-old skills are no longer locked in museums; they are being digitized, shared and reinvented online. Tradition and technology, once seen as opposites, are now powerful partners.
现代科技正在为传统文化打开新的大门。数百年的技艺不再锁在博物馆里；它们正被数字化、被分享、在线上被重新演绎。传统与科技曾被视为对立面，如今成了强大的伙伴。
② 主体段
First and foremost, digital technology preserves intangible cultural heritage before it fades: high-definition records and virtual exhibitions keep ancient crafts alive. What's more, short videos and livestreams bring traditional arts to millions of young viewers, turning passive audiences into active inheritors. Last but not least, when traditional patterns enter modern design and games, culture gains new life and even new markets.
首先，数字技术在非物质文化遗产消逝前将其保存下来：高清记录和虚拟展览让古老技艺保持生命力。其次，短视频和直播把传统艺术带到数百万年轻观众面前，把被动的观众变成主动的传承者。最后，当传统纹样进入现代设计和游戏，文化便获得了新的生命力，甚至打开了新的市场。
③ 结尾段
In conclusion, technology does not dilute tradition; it amplifies it. As young people, we should act as inheritors rather than bystanders, using the tools of our era to keep the old culture young.
总之，技术不会稀释传统，反而会放大传统。作为年轻人，我们应当做传承者而不是旁观者，用这个时代的工具让古老文化保持年轻。

② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, preserving intangible cultural heritage with digital technology yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in keeping ancient crafts alive and accessible. Chiefly among these benefits is the fresh energy young creators bring to old arts. Moreover, the creative revival of traditional culture is instrumental in promoting cultural confidence and creative industries. This not only revives ancient skills but also opens new markets for cultural products. Last but not least, telling old stories in new ways wins the hearts of the young generation, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，用数字技术保护非物质文化遗产会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在让古老技艺保持生命力、触手可及上。其中最主要的一点，是年轻创作者为古老艺术注入的新活力。此外，传统文化的创新复兴有助于促进文化自信与创意产业。这不仅让古老技艺重焕生机，还为文化产品打开新市场。最后同样重要的是，用新方式讲老故事能赢得年轻一代的心，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that the creative revival of traditional culture is of great importance. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on passing on our cultural roots. In the future, we should continue to combine tradition with modern innovation, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
总之，很明显，传统文化的创新复兴至关重要。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但永远不应放弃传承我们的文化根脉。未来，我们应继续把传统与现代创新结合，让它在中国文明的长河中更加闪亮。
