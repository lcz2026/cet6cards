① 开头段
As AI becomes part of school life, students need to learn how to use it wisely instead of depending on it too much. Using AI as a helper rather than a substitute is, therefore, of great necessity. The following factors can account for this issue.
随着人工智能逐渐融入校园生活，学生需要学会明智地使用它，而不是过度依赖它。因此，将人工智能作为辅助工具而非替代品，显得尤为必要。以下因素可以解释这一问题。
② 主体段
Firstly, due to the busy schedule of university life, college students — who are engaged in studying, club work and job hunting — are especially susceptible to omnipresent stress. Through handing routine tasks to AI, their long-standing pressure can be effectively reduced. Moreover, emotional factors do play an important role in learning with AI. When students work out the key steps by themselves, the positive emotion of achievement can be triggered. At last, using AI wisely protects our physical and mental health in the long run. A recent survey shows that students who keep this habit report lower stress and better sleep, because they are no longer pushed around by endless notifications.
首先，由于大学生活节奏紧张，忙于学业、社团工作和求职的大学生尤其容易受到无处不在的压力的影响。将日常任务交给人工智能处理，可以有效减轻他们长期承受的压力。此外，情感因素在借助人工智能学习的过程中确实发挥着重要作用。当学生自己理清关键步骤时，成就感这一积极情绪便会被激发出来。最后，从长远来看，明智地使用人工智能有助于保护我们的身心健康。最近一项调查显示，保持这一习惯的学生报告称压力更小、睡眠更好，因为他们不再被无休止的通知牵着走。
③ 结尾段
Admittedly, using AI wisely may take time and effort, but its long-term benefits are worth it. Once we spare no efforts to keep our own thinking alive, it can benefit not only us but also the whole class.
诚然，明智地使用人工智能可能需要付出时间和精力，但其长期收益是值得的。只要我们不遗余力地保持独立思考，它不仅能让我们受益，也能让整个班级受益。

② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, acquiring fundamental knowledge of AI use yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to working out key steps. Moreover, AI use is instrumental in promoting mental well-being. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of engaging with AI use cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，掌握AI 的使用的基础知识会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升个人前景与核心能力上。其中最主要的一点，是它对自己理清关键步骤有显著贡献。此外，AI 的使用有助于促进身心健康。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，参与AI 的使用的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。

简而言之，AI 的使用对整个班级的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续探索和发展AI 的使用，让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
