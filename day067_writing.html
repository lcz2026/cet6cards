① 开头 · 引题亮观点
In a rapidly changing world, lifelong learning has become a necessity rather than a choice. As far as I am concerned, keeping learning all the time is the key to a better life.
在快速变化的世界里，终身学习已不是一种选择，而是必需。在我看来，持续学习是通往更好生活的钥匙。
② 主体段 · 两论点+例子
First, knowledge is being updated faster than ever, so what we learn in school is far from enough. Only by learning new skills can we keep up with the times. What's more, learning itself brings joy. Many elderly people take up painting or music after retirement, which keeps their minds active and their lives colorful.
首先，知识更新的速度前所未有，我们在学校学的东西远远不够。只有学习新技能，才能跟上时代。此外，学习本身就带来快乐。许多老人退休后学画画、学音乐，这让他们的头脑保持活跃，生活丰富多彩。
③ 结尾 · 重申+展望
In conclusion, lifelong learning benefits us in many ways. Let's make learning a lifelong habit and embrace every change.
总之，终身学习在多方面让我们受益。让我们把学习变成一生的习惯，拥抱每一次改变。

② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, acquiring fundamental knowledge through lifelong learning yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to boosting employability and career advancement. Moreover, lifelong learning is instrumental in promoting academic progress and achievement. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of engaging with lifelong learning cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，通过终身学习掌握基础知识，会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升未来前景与核心能力上。其中最主要的一点，是它对提升就业竞争力与职业晋升有显著贡献。此外，终身学习有助于促进学业进步与成绩提升。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，终身学习的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that lifelong learning is of great importance for personal growth. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. In the future, we should continue to explore and develop lifelong learning, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
简而言之，终身学习对个人成长的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续探索和推进终身学习，让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
