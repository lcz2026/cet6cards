第 1 句
节俭（frugality）是中华民族的传统美德。
Frugality is a traditional virtue of the Chinese nation.
第 2 句
在中国古代，生产力低下，先人们深知劳动成果来之不易，因而秉持节俭的理念。
In ancient China, due to low productivity, our ancestors understood that the fruits of labor were hard-earned, and thus upheld the concept of thriftiness.
第 3 句
到了物质极大丰富的今天，中国人民依然坚持节俭的生活方式，体现了理性消费的生活理念和对劳动的尊重。
Today, despite great material abundance, the Chinese people still adhere to a frugal lifestyle, which reflects the philosophy of rational consumption and respect for labor.
第 4 句
近年来，中国政府持续加强节约型社会的建设，倡导“光盘行动”，减少食物浪费，提倡简约低碳的生活方式，推进资源循环利用，反对过度消费。
In recent years, the Chinese government has continuously strengthened the building of a conservation-oriented society, advocating for the "Clean Your Plate" campaign, reducing food waste, promoting a simple and low-carbon lifestyle, advancing resource recycling, and opposing excessive consumption.
第 5 句
节约型社会的建设有力地推动了中国经济的高质量发
The construction of a conservation-oriented society has powerfully promoted the high-quality development of China's economy.
