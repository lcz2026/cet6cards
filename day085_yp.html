📖 今日翻译押题 · 自古以来，中国的水资源北缺…
瑞斯拜押题 · 2026.12 热点 · 先动笔翻再对照，5 分钟过完
自古以来，中国的水资源北缺南丰，分布极不均衡。为了有效解决北方严重缺水问题，中国政府实施了南水北调工程（the South-to-North Water Diversion Project）。这是一项跨区域配置水资源的宏大水利工程。历经数十年的规划与筹备，工程于 2002 年开工建设，分为东、中、西三条线路，总长 4350 公里，惠及人口将超过 4 亿。自 2014 年通水以来，工程向北方调水累计已超 500 亿立方米，为北方地区的人民提供了可靠的水资源，同时也极大地改善了这一地区的生态环境，促进了经济的可持续健康发展。

第 1 句
自古以来，中国的水资源北缺南丰，分布极不均衡。
Since ancient times, China's water resources have been scarce in the north and abundant in the south, with an extremely uneven distribution.
第 2 句
为了有效解决北方严重缺水问题，中国政府实施了南水北调工程（the South-to-North Water Diversion Project）。
To effectively address the severe water shortage in the north, the Chinese government has implemented the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, a grand water conservancy project for cross-regional water resource allocation.
第 3 句
这是一项跨区域配置水资源的宏大水利工程。
After decades of planning and preparation, construction of the project began in 2002.
第 4 句
历经数十年的规划与筹备，工程于 2002 年开工建设，分为东、中、西三条线路，总长 4350 公里，惠及人口将超过 4 亿。
It consists of three routes (eastern, middle, and western) with a total length of 4,350 kilometers, benefiting over 400 million people.
第 5 句
自 2014 年通水以来，工程向北方调水累计已超 500 亿立方米，为北方地区的人民提供了可靠的水资源，同时也极大地改善了这一地区的生态环境，促进了经济的可持续健康发展。
Since water diversion started in 2014, the project has delivered more than 50 billion cubic meters of water to the north, providing a reliable water source for people in northern regions, significantly improving the ecological environment there, and promoting sustainable and healthy economic development.

✍️ 今日作文押题 · 中国梦与理想的实现
王江涛 12 月预测 · 指定首句作文（6 月三套同款命题形态）
指定首句：While striving for the Chinese dream, young people enjoy more opportunities to realize their ideals.
👆 范文开头段 · 点遮英文自测
① 开头段
The Chinese Dream is closely connected with the dreams of every young person. Our era offers unprecedented opportunities, from startup support to diverse educational paths. Yet the realization of any dream ultimately depends on our own struggle.
中国梦与每个年轻人的梦想紧密相连。我们的时代提供了前所未有的机遇，从创业扶持到多元化的教育路径。然而，任何梦想的实现最终都取决于我们自身的奋斗。

② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, acquiring fundamental knowledge of the realization of our personal dreams yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to the multiple platforms the era offers to young people. Moreover, the realization of our personal dreams is instrumental in promoting personal growth and social progress. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of engaging with the realization of our personal dreams cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，掌握实现个人梦想的基础知识会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升个人前景与核心能力上。其中最主要的一点，是它对时代为年轻人提供的多元平台有显著贡献。此外，实现个人梦想有助于促进个人成长与社会进步。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，参与实现个人梦想的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that the realization of our personal dreams is of great importance for working hard for our dreams. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. In the future, we should continue to explore and develop the realization of our personal dreams, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
简而言之，实现个人梦想对为梦想努力奋斗的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续探索和发展实现个人梦想，让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
