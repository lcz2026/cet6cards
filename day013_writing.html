① 开头 · 引题亮观点
Nowadays, whether college students should take part-time jobs has become a heated topic. As far as I am concerned, taking a suitable part-time job does students more good than harm.
如今，大学生该不该做兼职已成为热议话题。在我看来，做一份合适的兼职对学生利大于弊。
② 主体段 · 两论点+例子
First and foremost, a part-time job offers students valuable social experience. For example, a student who works as a shop assistant learns how to communicate with different people. What's more, earning money by themselves helps students understand the value of labor, so they will spend money more wisely. Besides, part-time jobs can also help students discover their real interests and plan their careers earlier.
首先，兼职能为学生提供宝贵的社会经验。例如，做过店员的学生能学会如何与不同的人沟通。此外，自己挣钱能让学生体会劳动的价值，从而更明智地消费。而且，兼职还能帮助学生发现自己的真正兴趣，更早地规划职业。
③ 结尾 · 重申+展望
In conclusion, as long as students can balance work and study, a part-time job is well worth a try. Only in this way can they prepare themselves for the future.
总之，只要学生能平衡好工作与学习，兼职就非常值得一试。只有这样，他们才能为未来做好准备。
