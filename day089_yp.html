✍️ 今日作文押题 · 数字素养：必备技能
王江涛 12 月预测 · 指定首句作文（6 月三套同款命题形态）
指定首句：Living in a sea of information, young people need digital literacy more than ever.
👆 范文开头段 · 点遮英文自测
① 开头段
Living in a sea of information, young people need digital literacy more than ever. Every day we receive countless messages, but not all of them deserve our trust. Knowing how to find, judge and use information has become a basic survival skill.
生活在信息的海洋中，年轻人比以往任何时候都更需要数字素养。我们每天接收无数信息，但并非所有信息都值得信任。知道如何查找、判断和使用信息，已成为一项基本的生存技能。

① 模板开头 · 首句照抄+亮观点
Living in a sea of information, young people need digital literacy more than ever. In an increasingly interconnected world, digital literacy stands out as an essential priority. It is of great necessity for students and young people to equip themselves with practical skills and experience. Reasons and concrete evidence to support my viewpoint are as follows.
数字素养：必备技能。（首句照抄）在这个联系日益紧密的世界里，数字素养脱颖而出，成为一项必不可少的优先事项。学生和年轻人有必要让自己掌握实用技能与经验。支持我观点的理由和具体证据如下。
② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, acquiring fundamental knowledge of digital literacy yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to the ability to check sources and question bias. Moreover, digital literacy is instrumental in promoting rational thinking and online safety. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of engaging with digital literacy cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，掌握数字素养的基础知识会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升个人前景与核心能力上。其中最主要的一点，是它对核查来源、质疑偏见的能力有显著贡献。此外，数字素养有助于促进理性思考与上网安全。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，参与数字素养的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that digital literacy is of great importance for careful and rational online habits. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. In the future, we should continue to explore and develop digital literacy, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
简而言之，数字素养对谨慎而理性的上网习惯的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续探索和发展数字素养，让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
