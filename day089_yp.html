长征是中国共产党领导的中国工农红军在1934年至1936年间进行的一次伟大战略转移。
The Long March was a great strategic transfer carried out between 1934 and 1936 by the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army under the leadership of the Communist Party of China.
第 2 句
长征途中，红军将士穿越了荒无人烟的雪山草地，跨越了十余个省，行程约两万五千里。
On the way, the Red Army soldiers crossed uninhabited snow-capped mountains and marshlands, passed through more than ten provinces and covered a distance of about 25,000 li.
第 3 句
长征的胜利，不仅保存了中国革命的有生力量，更铸就了伟大的长征精神。
The victory of the Long March not only preserved the effective forces of the Chinese revolution but also forged the great Long March spirit.
第 4 句
这种精神是中华民族百折不挠的民族精神的最高体现，也是激励中国人民不断前进的强大力量。
This spirit is the highest embodiment of the Chinese nation's indomitable character and a powerful force that inspires the Chinese people to keep moving forward.
第 5 句
如今，长征精神依然激励着中国人克服各种困难，为实现中华民族的伟大复兴而努力奋斗。
Today, the Long March spirit still inspires the Chinese people to overcome all kinds of difficulties and strive for the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation.

② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, acquiring fundamental knowledge of rational spending yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to buying something we really use. Moreover, rational spending is instrumental in promoting the simple lifestyle. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of engaging with rational spending cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，掌握理性消费的基础知识会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升个人前景与核心能力上。其中最主要的一点，是它对买真正用得上的东西有显著贡献。此外，理性消费有助于促进简约生活方式。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，参与理性消费的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that rational spending is of great importance for us and the planet. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. In the future, we should continue to explore and develop rational spending, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
简而言之，理性消费对我们自己与地球的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续探索和发展理性消费，让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
