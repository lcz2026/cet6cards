📖 今日翻译押题 · 近年来，人形机器人已成为全…
瑞斯拜12月版 · 2026.12 热点 · 先动笔翻再对照，5 分钟过完
近年来，人形机器人已成为全球科技竞争的新焦点。与普通工业机器人不同，人形机器人拥有与人类相似的外形和运动能力，能够适应为人类设计的各种环境和工具。在中国，人形机器人正加速从实验室走向实际应用，在养老陪护、危险环境作业、教育展示等场景中展现出巨大潜力。人形机器人的核心挑战在于运动控制、感知交互与能源续航等技术的深度融合。随着人工智能和高端制造技术的不断突破，人形机器人有望成为未来智能社会的重要组成部分，深刻改变人们的生产生活方式。

近年来，人形机器人已成为全球科技竞争的新焦点。
In recent years, humanoid robots have become a new focus of global technological competition.
第 2 句
与普通工业机器人不同，人形机器人拥有与人类相似的外形和运动能力，能够适应为人类设计的各种环境和工具。
Unlike ordinary industrial robots, humanoid robots have a human-like appearance and movement capability, enabling them to adapt to the various environments and tools designed for humans.
第 3 句
在中国，人形机器人正加速从实验室走向实际应用，在养老陪护、危险环境作业、教育展示等场景中展现出巨大潜力。
In China, humanoid robots are speeding up their transition from laboratories to practical applications, showing enormous potential in scenarios such as elderly care, operations in hazardous environments and educational exhibitions.
第 4 句
人形机器人的核心挑战在于运动控制、感知交互与能源续航等技术的深度融合。
The core challenge for humanoid robots lies in the deep integration of technologies such as motion control, perception and interaction, and energy endurance.
第 5 句
随着人工智能和高端制造技术的不断突破，人形机器人有望成为未来智能社会的重要组成部分，深刻改变人们的生产生活方式。
With continuous breakthroughs in artificial intelligence and high-end manufacturing, humanoid robots are expected to become an important part of the future intelligent society and to profoundly change the way people produce and live.

✍️ 今日作文押题 · 当传统文化遇上科技
王江涛 12 月预测 · 指定首句作文（6 月三套同款命题形态）
指定首句：In an Era of Artificial Intelligence, traditional Chinese culture is becoming increasingly crucial for our social development.
👆 范文开头段 · 点遮英文自测
① 开头段
Modern technology is opening new doors for traditional culture. Centuries-old skills are no longer locked in museums; they are being digitized, shared and reinvented online. Tradition and technology, once seen as opposites, are now powerful partners.
现代科技正在为传统文化打开新的大门。数百年的技艺不再锁在博物馆里；它们正被数字化、被分享、在线上被重新演绎。传统与科技曾被视为对立面，如今成了强大的伙伴。

① 模板开头 · 首句照抄+亮观点
In an Era of Artificial Intelligence, traditional Chinese culture is becoming increasingly crucial for our social development. In an increasingly interconnected world, the creative revival of traditional culture stands out as an essential priority. It is of great necessity for students and young people to equip themselves with practical skills and experience. Reasons and concrete evidence to support my viewpoint are as follows.
当传统文化遇上科技。（首句照抄）在这个联系日益紧密的世界里，传统文化的创新复兴脱颖而出，成为一项必不可少的优先事项。学生和年轻人有必要让自己掌握实用技能与经验。支持我观点的理由和具体证据如下。
② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, acquiring fundamental knowledge of the creative revival of traditional culture yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to the fresh energy young creators bring to old arts. Moreover, the creative revival of traditional culture is instrumental in promoting cultural confidence and creative industries. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of engaging with the creative revival of traditional culture cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，掌握传统文化的创新复兴的基础知识会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升个人前景与核心能力上。其中最主要的一点，是它对年轻创作者为古老艺术注入的新活力有显著贡献。此外，传统文化的创新复兴有助于促进文化自信与创意产业。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，参与传统文化的创新复兴的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that the creative revival of traditional culture is of great importance for passing on our cultural roots. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. In the future, we should continue to explore and develop the creative revival of traditional culture, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
简而言之，传统文化的创新复兴对传承我们的文化根脉的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续探索和发展传统文化的创新复兴，让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
