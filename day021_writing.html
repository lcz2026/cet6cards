① 开头 · 现象+亮观点
Nowadays, short videos and social media have taken up much of our attention. In my opinion, deep reading in the digital age is more important than ever before.
如今，短视频和社交媒体占据了我们大量注意力。在我看来，数字时代的深度阅读比以往任何时候都重要。
② 主体段 · 让步+对比论证
Admittedly, digital reading is fast and convenient, for we can get information anytime and anywhere. However, fragmented reading hardly trains our minds. Deep reading, by contrast, teaches us to think logically and to focus on one thing for a long time. It also helps us better understand others' feelings.
诚然，数字阅读快速便捷，我们随时随地都能获取信息。然而，碎片化阅读很难锻炼思维。相比之下，深度阅读教会我们逻辑思考、长时间专注于一件事，还能让我们更好地理解他人的感受。
③ 结尾 · 建议+展望
Therefore, why not set aside 30 minutes every day for a paper book? Deep reading will keep our minds sharp in this fast-changing digital world.
因此，何不每天留出 30 分钟读一本纸质书呢？在这个快速变化的数字世界，深度阅读会让我们的头脑保持敏锐。
