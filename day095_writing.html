① 开头段
As artificial intelligence grows more powerful, academic integrity faces a new test. When an essay can be generated in seconds, the line between learning and copying becomes blurred. In my view, how we use AI defines who we really are as learners.
随着人工智能日益强大，学术诚信面临新的考验。当一篇文章能在几秒钟内生成，学习与抄袭的界限就变得模糊。在我看来，我们如何使用 AI，决定了我们作为学习者真正的样子。
② 主体段
First and foremost, honesty is the foundation of education: degrees and grades lose their meaning if they are not earned by real work. What's more, over-reliance on AI stifles critical thinking, leaving students with polished texts but empty minds. However, using AI properly is not cheating—asking it for feedback or explanations deepens our understanding, as long as the final work remains our own.
首先，诚实是教育的基石：如果学位和成绩不是通过真实的努力获得的，它们就失去了意义。其次，过度依赖 AI 会抑制批判性思维，让学生只剩华丽的文本却没有充实的头脑。不过，正确使用 AI 并不是作弊——只要最终成果出自自己之手，让 AI 提供反馈或讲解反而能加深理解。
③ 结尾段
In conclusion, technology changes our tools, but it should never change our principles. Let AI assist our thinking, not replace it, so that integrity remains the true mark of every learner.
总之，技术改变的是我们的工具，但永远不该改变我们的原则。让 AI 辅助我们的思考，而不是替代它，诚信才会永远是每个学习者真正的标志。
