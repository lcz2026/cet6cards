① 开头段
Stepping out of our comfort zone often feels uncomfortable before it feels rewarding. Trying new things is, therefore, of great necessity. The following factors can account for this issue.
走出舒适区，往往先让人感到不适，而后才体会到收获。因此，尝试新事物至关重要。以下因素可以解释这一现象。
② 主体段
Firstly, owing to the prosperity of China's economy, the whole country is promoting innovation among young people. Therefore, the courage to try unfamiliar things is increasingly valued. Moreover, emotional factors do play an important role in personal growth. When students speak in public for the first time and get through it, the positive emotion of achievement can be triggered. At last, facing challenges protects our physical and mental health in the long run. A recent survey shows that students who keep this habit report lower stress and better sleep, because they are no longer held back by the fear of failure.
首先，得益于中国经济的繁荣，全国上下都在鼓励年轻人创新。因此，敢于尝试陌生事物的勇气越来越受到重视。此外，情感因素在个人成长中确实发挥着重要作用。当学生第一次公开演讲并成功完成时，成就感这种积极情绪便会被激发出来。最后，从长远来看，迎接挑战有助于维护身心健康。最近一项调查显示，保持这一习惯的学生压力更小、睡眠更好，因为他们不再被对失败的恐惧所束缚。
③ 结尾段
Admittedly, stepping out of the comfort zone may take time and effort, but its long-term benefits are worth it. Once we spare no efforts to accept one small challenge each week, it can benefit not only us but also the people around us.
诚然，走出舒适区可能需要付出时间和精力，但其长远收益值得。只要我们不遗余力地每周接受一项小挑战，它不仅能让我们受益，也能让身边的人受益。
