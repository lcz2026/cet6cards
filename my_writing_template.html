📌 使用方法
适配题型"X 的重要性 / 为什么 X 很关键"——六级最高频观点论证题。尤其适配个人成长类：终身学习、阅读、运动、实习、合作、时间管理。
考场流程审题圈出主题词 → 开头段换词默写（约 1 分钟）→ 主体段走 Primarily / Moreover / Last but not least 三层 → 结尾展望收束。全篇约 200 词，正好卡六级要求。
先背框架词In an increasingly interconnected world / stands out as an essential priority / It is of great necessity / are as follows / yields immense benefits / is instrumental in / not only…but also / lays a solid foundation / Last but not least / In a nutshell / Despite the difficulties and challenges / the long river of human civilization
⚠️ 社会类话题（环保/科技/文化）替换句：中间段的就业学业两句不适配，换这两句—— Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to social well-being and sustainable development. 其中最主要的是，它对社会福祉与可持续发展有显著贡献。 This not only enhances the public's awareness of joint responsibility but also fosters a healthier and more harmonious society. 这不仅增强了公众共同担责的意识，也促进了一个更健康、更和谐的社会。
