① 开头段
Before buying something new, it is helpful to ask whether we will really use it. Thinking twice before paying is, therefore, of great necessity. The following factors can account for this issue.
在购买新东西之前，问问自己是否真的会用到它，是很有帮助的。因此，付款前三思而后行十分必要。以下因素可以解释这一点。
② 主体段
Firstly, with the increasing awareness of environmental protection, more and more people start to realize that it is more environmentally friendly to buy only what we truly need. As a result, the simple lifestyle is becoming popular among young people. Moreover, emotional factors do play an important role in rational spending. When students buy something they really use, the positive emotion of satisfaction can be triggered. At last, buying wisely protects our physical and mental health in the long run. A recent survey shows that students who keep this habit report lower stress and better sleep, because they are no longer pushed around by endless notifications.
首先，随着环保意识的增强，越来越多的人开始意识到，只购买真正需要的东西更环保。因此，简约生活方式在年轻人中越来越受欢迎。此外，情感因素在理性消费中确实发挥着重要作用。当学生购买真正需要的东西时，满足感等积极情绪便会油然而生。最后，从长远来看，明智消费有助于保护我们的身心健康。最近一项调查显示，保持这一习惯的学生压力更小、睡眠更好，因为他们不再被无休止的促销活动牵着走。
③ 结尾段
Admittedly, thinking twice may take time and effort, but its long-term benefits are worth it. Once we spare no efforts to ask that one question before each order, it can benefit not only us but also the planet.
诚然，三思而后行可能需要花费时间和精力，但其长远益处值得。一旦我们每次下单前都尽力问自己这个问题，它不仅能让我们受益，也能让地球受益。
