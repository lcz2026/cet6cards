① 开头段
In the information age, the value of the humanities deserves renewed attention. While technology determines how fast we run, the humanities decide where we are heading. Ignoring them would leave our society efficient but directionless.
在信息时代，人文学科的价值值得重新审视。技术决定我们跑得多快，而人文学科决定我们走向何方。忽视它们，我们的社会将变得高效却迷失方向。
② 主体段
First and foremost, the humanities cultivate critical thinking and communication skills—precisely the soft skills that machines cannot replace. What's more, literature, history and philosophy strengthen our empathy and inclusion, helping us understand different people and cultures. Last but not least, in the AI era, questions about privacy, algorithmic bias and ethical boundaries can only be answered with humanistic wisdom.
首先，人文学科培养批判性思维和沟通能力——这正是机器无法替代的软技能。其次，文学、历史和哲学增强我们的同理心与包容性，帮助我们理解不同的人与文化。最后，在 AI 时代，关于隐私、算法偏见和伦理边界的问题，只有依靠人文智慧才能回答。
③ 结尾段
In conclusion, the humanities are not a luxury but a necessity in the information age. Technology builds our capacity, while the humanities build our compass; only when the two combine can progress truly serve humanity.
总之，在信息时代，人文学科不是奢侈品而是必需品。技术造就我们的能力，人文造就我们的指南针；两者结合，进步才能真正服务于人类。

② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, acquiring fundamental knowledge of liberal arts yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to broadening our view of humanity. Moreover, liberal arts is instrumental in promoting critical thinking and empathy. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of engaging with liberal arts cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，掌握文科（人文学科）的基础知识会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升个人前景与核心能力上。其中最主要的一点，是它对拓宽我们对人的理解有显著贡献。此外，文科（人文学科）有助于促进批判性思维与同理心。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，参与文科（人文学科）的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。

In a nutshell, it is clear that liberal arts is of great importance for students and the whole society. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. 简而言之，文科（人文学科）对学生与整个社会的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续探索和发展文科（人文学科），让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
