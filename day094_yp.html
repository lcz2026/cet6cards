📖 今日翻译押题 · 县城在中国城乡发展中承担着…
瑞斯拜12月版 · 2026.12 热点 · 先动笔翻再对照，5 分钟过完
县城在中国城乡发展中承担着连接周边乡村与较大城市的作用。农产品在这里集散，居民也能在县城获得教育、医疗和商业服务。与单纯扩大建筑面积相比，完善就业机会和公共设施更能增强县城的吸引力。一些地方根据当地产业特点发展加工、物流和技术培训，使乡村居民不必远离家庭也能获得工作。与此同时，县城发展需要把人口变化、财政能力和居民实际需求结合起来，形成能够长期维持的建设与管理方式。

第 1 句
县城在中国城乡发展中承担着连接周边乡村与较大城市的作用。
County towns play a role in China's urban-rural development by connecting the surrounding countryside with larger cities.
第 2 句
农产品在这里集散，居民也能在县城获得教育、医疗和商业服务。
Agricultural products are gathered and distributed here, and residents can also obtain education, medical care and commercial services in county towns.
第 3 句
与单纯扩大建筑面积相比，完善就业机会和公共设施更能增强县城的吸引力。
Compared with simply expanding building areas, improving employment opportunities and public facilities is more effective in enhancing the appeal of county towns.
第 4 句
一些地方根据当地产业特点发展加工、物流和技术培训，使乡村居民不必远离家庭也能获得工作。
Some places have developed processing, logistics and technical training in line with local industrial features, enabling rural residents to find work without moving far from their families.
第 5 句
与此同时，县城发展需要把人口变化、财政能力和居民实际需求结合起来，形成能够长期维持的建设与管理方式。
At the same time, the development of county towns requires combining population changes, fiscal capacity and the actual needs of residents, so as to form approaches to construction and management that can be sustained over the long term.

① 模板开头 · 首句照抄+亮观点
Stepping out of our comfort zone often feels uncomfortable before it feels rewarding. In an increasingly interconnected world, trying new things stands out as an essential priority. It is of great necessity for students and young people to equip themselves with practical skills and experience. Reasons and concrete evidence to support my viewpoint are as follows.
走出舒适区（瑞斯拜12月⑤）。（首句照抄）在这个联系日益紧密的世界里，尝试新事物脱颖而出，成为一项必不可少的优先事项。学生和年轻人有必要让自己掌握实用技能与经验。支持我观点的理由和具体证据如下。
② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, acquiring fundamental knowledge of trying new things yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to speaking in public for the first time. Moreover, trying new things is instrumental in promoting personal growth. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of engaging with trying new things cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，掌握尝试新事物的基础知识会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升个人前景与核心能力上。其中最主要的一点，是它对第一次公开演讲有显著贡献。此外，尝试新事物有助于促进个人成长。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，参与尝试新事物的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that trying new things is of great importance for the people around us. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. In the future, we should continue to explore and develop trying new things, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
简而言之，尝试新事物对身边的人的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续探索和发展尝试新事物，让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
