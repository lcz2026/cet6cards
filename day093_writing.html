① 开头段
In the age of artificial intelligence, critical thinking has become more valuable than ever. Developing critical thinking ability is, therefore, of great necessity. The following factors can account for this issue.
在人工智能时代，批判性思维比以往任何时候都更有价值。因此，发展批判性思维能力至关重要。以下因素可以解释这一现象。
② 主体段
Firstly, owing to the prosperity of China's economy, the whole country is promoting the education of independent thinking. Therefore, critical thinking is increasingly valued in almost every field. Moreover, emotional factors do play an important role in critical thinking. When students question an answer and finally find the truth, the positive emotion of achievement can be triggered. At last, thinking critically protects our physical and mental health in the long run. A recent survey shows that students who keep this habit report lower stress and better sleep, because they are no longer pushed around by endless notifications.
首先，随着中国经济的繁荣，全国都在推进独立思考教育。因此，批判性思维在几乎所有领域都越来越受到重视。此外，情感因素在批判性思维中确实发挥着重要作用。当学生质疑一个答案并最终发现真相时，成就感这一积极情绪便会被激发出来。最后，从长远来看，批判性思维有助于保护我们的身心健康。最近一项调查显示，保持这一习惯的学生报告称压力更小、睡眠更好，因为他们不再被无休止的通知牵着走。
③ 结尾段
Admittedly, thinking critically may take time and effort, but its long-term benefits are worth it. Once we spare no efforts to check before we believe, it can benefit not only us but also the whole society.
诚然，批判性思维可能需要付出时间和精力，但其长期收益是值得的。一旦我们竭尽全力，在相信之前先加以核实，它不仅能造福我们自身，也能造福整个社会。

In the age of artificial intelligence, critical thinking has become more valuable than ever. In an increasingly interconnected world, critical thinking stands out as an essential priority. It is of great necessity for students and young people to equip themselves with practical skills and experience. Reasons and concrete evidence to support my viewpoint are as follows.
AI 时代批判性思维的重要性（瑞斯拜12月③）。（首句照抄）在这个联系日益紧密的世界里，批判性思维脱颖而出，成为一项必不可少的优先事项。学生和年轻人有必要让自己掌握实用技能与经验。支持我观点的理由和具体证据如下。
② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, acquiring fundamental knowledge of critical thinking yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to questioning an answer and finding the truth. Moreover, critical thinking is instrumental in promoting independent thinking. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of engaging with critical thinking cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，掌握批判性思维的基础知识会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升个人前景与核心能力上。其中最主要的一点，是它对质疑答案并找到真相有显著贡献。此外，批判性思维有助于促进独立思考。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，参与批判性思维的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that critical thinking is of great importance for the whole society. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. In the future, we should continue to explore and develop critical thinking, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
简而言之，批判性思维对整个社会的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续探索和发展批判性思维，让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
