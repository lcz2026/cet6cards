① 开头段
Economic development and environmental protection are not enemies but partners. In the past, many countries grew rich at the cost of clear skies and clean rivers. Today, sustainable development shows us a wiser path.
经济发展与环境保护不是敌人，而是伙伴。过去，许多国家以失去蓝天清河为代价致富。今天，可持续发展为我们指明了一条更明智的道路。
② 主体段
First and foremost, green development protects the very resources that the economy depends on, such as water, land and energy. What's more, new industries—renewable energy, electric vehicles and the circular economy—are creating millions of jobs while reducing pollution. Last but not least, as individuals, we can also contribute through low-carbon lifestyles: taking public transport, saving energy and sorting waste.
首先，绿色发展保护了经济所依赖的根本资源，如水、土地和能源。其次，新能源、电动汽车和循环经济等新产业在减少污染的同时创造了数百万就业岗位。最后，作为个人，我们也可以通过低碳生活方式作出贡献：乘坐公共交通、节约能源、垃圾分类。
③ 结尾段
In conclusion, protecting the environment is not a brake on development but its engine. Only by walking the green path can we leave both prosperity and beauty to future generations.
总之，保护环境不是发展的刹车，而是发展的引擎。只有走绿色之路，我们才能把繁荣与美丽一并留给子孙后代。

② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, developing the economy in a green way yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in protecting the resources we depend on. Chiefly among these benefits is the millions of jobs created by new green industries. Moreover, the green path of development is instrumental in promoting sustainable development and social well-being. This not only reduces pollution but also upgrades the whole economy. Last but not least, low-carbon lifestyles of every individual build a greener society, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，以绿色方式发展经济会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在保护我们所依赖的资源上。其中最主要的一点，是绿色新产业创造的数百万就业岗位。此外，绿色发展之路有助于促进可持续发展与社会福祉。这不仅减少污染，还升级整个经济结构。最后同样重要的是，每个人的低碳生活方式构建更绿色的社会，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that the green path of development is of great importance. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on protecting our environment. In the future, we should continue to walk the green path of development, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
总之，很明显，绿色发展之路至关重要。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但永远不应放弃保护我们的环境。未来，我们应继续坚持走绿色发展之路，让它在中国文明的长河中更加闪亮。
