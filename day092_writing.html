① 开头段
In a fast-paced society, the ability to focus deeply has become a rare and valuable skill. Training this ability is, therefore, of great necessity. The following factors can account for this issue.
在快节奏的社会中，深度专注的能力已成为一种稀缺而宝贵的技能。因此，培养这种能力十分必要。以下因素可以解释这一问题。
② 主体段
Firstly, due to the busy schedule of university life, college students — who are engaged in studying, club work and job hunting — are especially susceptible to omnipresent stress. Through focusing on one task at a time, their long-standing pressure can be effectively reduced. Moreover, emotional factors do play an important role in deep work. When students finish a difficult chapter in one sitting, the positive emotion of achievement can be triggered. At last, deep focus protects our physical and mental health in the long run. A recent survey shows that students who keep this habit report lower stress and better sleep, because they are no longer pushed around by endless notifications.
首先，由于大学生活节奏紧张，忙于学业、社团工作和求职的大学生尤其容易受到无处不在的压力影响。通过一次只专注于一项任务，他们长期积累的压力可以得到有效缓解。此外，情绪因素在深度工作中确实发挥着重要作用。当学生一次性完成一章难度较大的内容时，成就感等积极情绪便会被激发出来。最后，从长远来看，深度专注有助于保护我们的身心健康。最近一项调查显示，保持这一习惯的学生报告称压力更小、睡眠更好，因为他们不再被无休止的通知牵着走。
③ 结尾段
Admittedly, training deep focus may take time and effort, but its long-term benefits are worth it. Once we spare no efforts to guard one quiet hour each day, it can benefit not only us but also our future careers.
诚然，培养深度专注可能需要时间和努力，但其长期收益是值得的。只要我们不遗余力地守护每天一段安静的时光，它不仅能让我们受益，也将惠及我们的未来职业生涯。
