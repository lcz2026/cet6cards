① 开头 · 设问亮观点
With smartphones in every pocket, technology has entered every corner of our life. Is it a blessing or a curse? Different people hold different opinions, but I believe it depends on how we use it.
随着智能手机人手一部，科技已进入我们生活的每个角落。它是福还是祸？不同的人看法不同，但我认为这取决于我们如何使用它。
② 主体段 · 一利一弊
On the one hand, technology brings great convenience. We pay with a scan of a QR code, and we attend classes online at home. On the other hand, it also causes problems: our personal information may be leaked, and people spend less time talking face to face.
一方面，科技带来极大便利。我们扫码支付，在家上网课。另一方面，它也带来问题：个人信息可能泄露，人们面对面交流的时间变少了。
③ 结尾 · 点破本质
In conclusion, technology itself is neutral. It is a blessing when we use it wisely, and a curse when we let it control our life. The choice is in our own hands.
总之，科技本身是中立的。明智使用，它就是福；任它掌控生活，它就是祸。选择权在我们自己手中。

② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, mastering modern tools yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to boosting employability and career advancement. Moreover, using technology wisely is instrumental in promoting academic progress and achievement: with smartphones and online courses, knowledge is no longer beyond our reach. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of engaging with technology rationally cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，掌握现代工具会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升未来前景与核心能力上。其中最主要的一点，是它对提升就业竞争力与职业晋升有显著贡献。此外，明智地使用科技有助于促进学业进步与成绩提升：有了智能手机和网课，知识不再是遥不可及的东西。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，理性使用科技的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that using technology wisely is of great importance for both study and life. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. In the future, we should continue to explore and develop technology in a rational way, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
简而言之，明智用科技对学习和生活的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应以理性的方式继续探索和发展科技，让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
