① 开头 · 引题亮观点
In a rapidly changing world, lifelong learning has become a necessity rather than a choice. As far as I am concerned, keeping learning all the time is the key to a better life.
在快速变化的世界里，终身学习已不是一种选择，而是必需。在我看来，持续学习是通往更好生活的钥匙。
② 主体段 · 两论点+例子
First, knowledge is being updated faster than ever, so what we learn in school is far from enough. Only by learning new skills can we keep up with the times. What's more, learning itself brings joy. Many elderly people take up painting or music after retirement, which keeps their minds active and their lives colorful.
首先，知识更新的速度前所未有，我们在学校学的东西远远不够。只有学习新技能，才能跟上时代。此外，学习本身就带来快乐。许多老人退休后学画画、学音乐，这让他们的头脑保持活跃，生活丰富多彩。
③ 结尾 · 重申+展望
In conclusion, lifelong learning benefits us in many ways. Let's make learning a lifelong habit and embrace every change.
总之，终身学习在多方面让我们受益。让我们把学习变成一生的习惯，拥抱每一次改变。
