① 开头段
Artificial intelligence is reshaping the way we learn and live. From homework helpers to instant translation, AI has become a study partner for many students. In my view, AI should serve as a learning aid rather than a shortcut, and only responsible use can turn it into a real friend of learning.
人工智能正在重塑我们的学习和生活方式。从作业助手到即时翻译，AI 已成为许多学生的学习伙伴。在我看来，AI 应该是学习辅助工具而不是捷径，只有负责任地使用它，才能让它成为学习真正的朋友。
② 主体段
First and foremost, AI helps us learn more efficiently by explaining difficult points and offering personalized practice. What's more, it frees us from repetitive tasks so that we can focus on creative and critical thinking. However, if we simply copy answers from AI, we may lose the ability to think independently, and academic integrity will be at risk.
首先，AI 通过讲解难点和提供个性化练习，帮助我们更高效地学习。其次，它把我们从重复劳动中解放出来，让我们能专注于创造性思维和批判性思维。然而，如果我们只是从 AI 上抄答案，就可能丧失独立思考的能力，学术诚信也会面临风险。
③ 结尾段
In conclusion, AI is a tool, not a substitute for our own effort. We should set clear boundaries for its use, stay honest in our work, and keep our minds active. Only in this way can technology truly empower our growth.
总之，AI 只是工具，不能替代我们自身的努力。我们应该为它的使用设定清晰的边界，在学业中保持诚实，并让大脑保持活跃。只有这样，技术才能真正为我们的成长赋能。
