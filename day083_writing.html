① 开头 · 引谚语亮观点
As the saying goes, "Teamwork makes the dream work." No one can achieve great success alone, so learning to cooperate with others is a lesson we must learn.
俗话说：“团队合作成就梦想。”没有人能独自取得巨大成功，因此学会与他人合作是我们的必修课。
② 主体段 · 正反+举例
On the one hand, everyone has strengths and weaknesses. In a team, members can complement each other, so one plus one is often more than two. On the other hand, those who work alone are limited by their own abilities. Take a basketball game as an example: no star player can win without the passing and defense of his teammates.
一方面，人人都有长处和短处。在团队中，成员可以互补，一加一往往大于二。另一方面，单打独斗的人会被自身能力所限制。以篮球赛为例：没有队友的传球和防守，再耀眼的球星也无法赢球。
③ 结尾 · 重申+呼吁
In conclusion, teamwork is not only the key to success but also a core quality in the workplace. Let's learn to trust our partners and grow together.
总之，团队合作不仅是成功的钥匙，也是职场的核心素质。让我们学会信任伙伴，共同成长。

① 模板开头 · 题干句+亮观点
Teamwork makes the dream work. In an increasingly interconnected world, teamwork stands out as an essential priority. It is of great necessity for us to learn how to cooperate with different people. Reasons and concrete evidence to support my viewpoint are as follows.
团队合作成就梦想。（题干句照抄）在这个联系日益紧密的世界里，团队合作脱颖而出，成为一项必不可少的优先事项。学会与不同的人合作，是大有必要的。支持我观点的理由和具体证据如下。
② 模板主体 · 三层理由
Primarily, working with others yields immense benefits on a personal level, particularly in enhancing one's future prospects and core competencies. Chiefly among these benefits is its significant contribution to boosting employability and career advancement: teamwork is a core quality in the workplace, and no project can be completed by a single hero. Moreover, cooperation is instrumental in promoting academic progress and achievement, because members in a team can complement each other and one plus one is often more than two. This not only increases one's chances of securing desirable positions but also lays a solid foundation for long-term professional growth and higher earning potential. Last but not least, the process of trusting our partners cultivates greater self-confidence and a sense of personal competence, empowering individuals to tackle future challenges with greater assurance.
首先，与他人共事会在个人层面带来巨大收益，尤其体现在提升未来前景与核心能力上。其中最主要的一点，是它对提升就业竞争力与职业晋升有显著贡献：团队合作是职场的核心素质，没有任何项目能靠孤胆英雄完成。此外，合作有助于促进学业进步与成绩提升，因为团队成员可以互补，一加一往往大于二。这不仅增加了获得理想职位的机会，也为长期职业发展和更高的收入潜力打下坚实基础。最后同样重要的是，信任伙伴的过程能培养更强的自信心和个人胜任感，让人更有把握地应对未来的挑战。
③ 模板结尾 · 总结展望
In a nutshell, it is clear that teamwork is of great importance for both study and work. Despite the difficulties and challenges we may encounter, we should never give up on it. In the future, we should continue to explore and develop the spirit of cooperation, making it shine more brightly in the long river of human civilization.
简而言之，团队合作对学习和工作的重要性显而易见。尽管我们可能遇到困难和挑战，但绝不应放弃。未来，我们应继续培养和发展合作精神，让它在人类文明的长河中绽放出更加耀眼的光芒。
