尊老是中华民族的传统美德，深深植根于中国人的思想和行为中，是人们普遍遵守的行为规范和社会准则。在当今的中国，这种美德得到广泛传承。社会各界积极营造敬老助老的社会氛围。为老年人提供便利服务已成为社会共识，例如社区专门开设长者食堂，公共场所配置优先座位。政府还出台了一系列政策，为老年人的权益提供有力保障。尊老是社会和谐与发展的重要基石，有助于培育良好的社会氛围，推动整个社会文明进步。
👆 点这里遮住英文 · 对照整段回忆
参考译文（整段对照）
Respecting the elderly is a traditional virtue of the Chinese nation, deeply rooted in the thoughts and behaviors of the Chinese people. It is a widely observed code of conduct and social norm. In today's China, this virtue is being broadly passed down. All sectors of society are actively creating an atmosphere of respecting and assisting the elderly. Providing convenient services for seniors has become a social consensus, such as the establishment of community senior canteens and the allocation of priority seating in public places. Furthermore, the government has introduced a series of policies to provide a strong guarantee for the rights and interests of the elderly. Respecting the aged is an important cornerstone of social harmony and development, contributing to a positive social atmosphere and the civilizational progress of society as a whole.
