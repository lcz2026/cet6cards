第 1 句
China is rich in cultural heritage, and many traditional festivals and folk customs have been passed down and are still widely practiced today.

第 2 句
In recent years, various measures have been taken across the country to protect historical buildings, preserve traditional craftsmanship, and integrate traditional festivals with modern tourism.
近年来，各地采取了多种措施保护历史建筑、传承传统技艺，并将传统节庆与现代旅游相结合。
第 3 句
These efforts have brought new vitality to traditional culture, which is playing an increasingly important role in contemporary society.
这些努力使传统文化焕发出新的活力，在当代社会发挥着越来越重要的作用。
